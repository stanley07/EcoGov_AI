# GovOS AI & EcoGov AI — User Experience & Journeys (docs/02_user_experience.md)

This document specifies the user personas and detailed operational workflows for the GovOS platform and its environmental module, EcoGov AI.

---

## 1. User Personas (14 Types)

Here we define the target personas interacting with the GovOS/EcoGov ecosystem.

### 1.1 Commissioner or Agency Chairman

- **Objectives**: Demonstrate environmental regulatory progress to the public, optimize department budgets, and ensure policy alignment.
- **Responsibilities**: Reviews high-level performance metrics, authorizes major enforcement cases, and presents reporting data to governors or ministers.
- **Pain Points**: Legacy systems provide no real-time transparency; reports are manual, outdated, and prone to manipulation.
- **Permissions**: Read-only access to all tenant data; Write access to tenant policies and final enforcement approvals.
- **Information Needs**: High-level compliance indices, revenue collections, backlog statuses, and geographical risk distributions.
- **Workflow Interactions**: Views Executive Dashboard monthly; signs off on critical enforcement notices.
- **AI Interactions**: Executive Intelligence Agent updates summaries and responds to natural language queries on environmental trends.
- **Trust Concerns**: AI hallucinations could result in embarrassing public reporting or legal liabilities.
- **Success Measures**: 30% increase in fines collected; 20% reduction in average regional response times.

### 1.2 Permanent Secretary

- **Objectives**: Oversee civil service operations, verify compliance with regional laws, and coordinate inter-departmental efforts.
- **Responsibilities**: Evaluates operational budgets, signs off on policy adaptations, and manages administrative escalations.
- **Pain Points**: Slow paperwork cycles and departmental bottlenecks limit productivity.
- **Permissions**: Broad system configuration read permissions, workflow override capabilities.
- **Information Needs**: Departmental task queues, processing time metrics, budget expenditures.
- **Workflow Interactions**: Reviews escalated cases, audits inspector activities.
- **AI Interactions**: Prompted by Workflow Coordination Agent on stalled operations.
- **Trust Concerns**: Security of private government data within cloud environments.
- **Success Measures**: 50% decrease in document processing times.

### 1.3 Director (Environmental Protection Department)

- **Objectives**: Meet regulatory targets and ensure that inspections are conducted fairly and systematically.
- **Responsibilities**: Assigns inspection tasks to supervisors, reviews audit summaries, and signs off on compliance notices.
- **Pain Points**: Understaffed teams are overwhelmed by complaints; cannot easily monitor inspector field performance.
- **Permissions**: Read-write access to cases, inspection tasks, and staff assignments.
- **Information Needs**: Inspector locations, case volumes, facility compliance history.
- **Workflow Interactions**: Approves inspection schedules and assigns cases to inspectors.
- **AI Interactions**: Receives dashboard insights from Risk Assessment Agent regarding high-risk facilities.
- **Trust Concerns**: AI scoring might bias inspection schedules against specific businesses.
- **Success Measures**: 100% of high-risk facilities inspected annually.

### 1.4 Supervisor (Inspection / Compliance Team)

- **Objectives**: Manage day-to-day inspection activities and verify that field reports are accurate.
- **Responsibilities**: Approves inspection plans, reviews drafted reports, and monitors task queues.
- **Pain Points**: Spent hours rewriting poor-quality report drafts submitted by inspectors.
- **Permissions**: Read-write access to inspections, reports, and evidence.
- **Information Needs**: Daily inspection logs, field evidence status, inspector notes.
- **Workflow Interactions**: Reviews report drafts and approves recommendations for compliance actions.
- **AI Interactions**: Edits AI-drafted reports generated by the Inspection Report Drafting Agent.
- **Trust Concerns**: Worried AI report drafting will miss critical technical nuances.
- **Success Measures**: Average report approval time reduced from 3 days to under 1 hour.

### 1.5 Inspector (Field Officer)

- **Objectives**: Conduct thorough field inspections and collect clear compliance evidence quickly.
- **Responsibilities**: Visits facilities, completes digital checklists, uploads photos/videos, and drafts notes.
- **Pain Points**: Writing detailed reports in the office after a long day in the field; carrying paper forms in bad weather.
- **Permissions**: Read-write access to assigned inspections and evidence uploads.
- **Information Needs**: Facility location, routing directions, compliance history, and relevant checklist items.
- **Workflow Interactions**: Executes checklists on mobile-responsive UI and uploads evidence.
- **AI Interactions**: Dictates notes into app; uses Evidence Analysis Agent to verify photo clarity.
- **Trust Concerns**: Fear that AI will replace inspectors or flag minor human errors as failures.
- **Success Measures**: Number of inspections conducted per day increased by 50%.

### 1.6 Complaint Intake Officer

- **Objectives**: Register citizen complaints quickly and accurately.
- **Responsibilities**: Inputs phone-in or walk-in complaints into the database; triages public web portal complaints.
- **Pain Points**: Filtering out spam, duplicate, or irrelevant complaints (e.g., neighbor disputes).
- **Permissions**: Write access to complaints.
- **Information Needs**: Location details, incident descriptions, caller contact details.
- **Workflow Interactions**: Submits complaint records to the triage queue.
- **AI Interactions**: Uses Complaint Triage Agent to classify and check for duplicate submissions.
- **Trust Concerns**: AI might misclassify a severe issue as a low priority.
- **Success Measures**: Triage backlog eliminated; time-to-register under 2 minutes.

### 1.7 Compliance Officer

- **Objectives**: Monitor remediation timelines and verify that facilities address violations.
- **Responsibilities**: Reviews remediation submissions, issues extensions, and refers unresolved violations to legal.
- **Pain Points**: Tracking compliance deadlines across hundreds of facilities using spreadsheets.
- **Permissions**: Read-write access to remediation plans, notices, and compliance scores.
- **Information Needs**: Due dates, facility upload evidence, historic violation patterns.
- **Workflow Interactions**: Evaluates facility compliance submissions; updates remediation statuses.
- **AI Interactions**: Compliance Recommendation Agent provides structured next-step suggestions.
- **Trust Concerns**: System automated alerts might send premature notices to facilities.
- **Success Measures**: 80% remediation completion rate within the initial notice period.

### 1.8 Legal / Enforcement Officer

- **Objectives**: Build legally airtight prosecution files and collect unpaid regulatory fines.
- **Responsibilities**: Prepares enforcement actions, represents the agency, and drafts formal notices.
- **Pain Points**: Missing chain of custody for field photos, making evidence inadmissible in court.
- **Permissions**: Read access to all cases; Write access to formal legal notices and cases referred to enforcement.
- **Information Needs**: Cryptographic signatures of evidence, inspection history, signed inspector logs.
- **Workflow Interactions**: Marks cases as referred to court; uploads legal records.
- **AI Interactions**: Document Intelligence Agent extracts metadata from historical legal cases.
- **Trust Concerns**: AI-generated legal briefs could contain incorrect legal precedents.
- **Success Measures**: 95% legal success rate in contested enforcement actions.

### 1.9 Tenant Administrator (Agency IT Staff)

- **Objectives**: Maintain high system availability and manage organization-specific settings.
- **Responsibilities**: Configures roles, workflows, inspection checklists, and tenant metadata.
- **Pain Points**: Software changes require coding or expensive vendor interventions.
- **Permissions**: Full administrative access inside a single tenant partition.
- **Information Needs**: User access logs, API usage stats, configuration errors.
- **Workflow Interactions**: Manages user accounts and edits JSON checklist configurations.
- **AI Interactions**: Validates configuration syntax with help from Workflow Coordination Agent.
- **Trust Concerns**: Security vulnerabilities in third-party integrations.
- **Success Measures**: Zero software downtime and zero unauthorized access events.

### 1.10 Environmental Consultant

- **Objectives**: Help client facilities secure permits and maintain compliance with local laws.
- **Responsibilities**: Conducts pre-audits, uploads regulatory documents, and submits remediation reports.
- **Pain Points**: Waiting weeks to receive permit status updates from government offices.
- **Permissions**: Read-write access only to client facilities they represent.
- **Information Needs**: Permit requirements, checklist status, government feedback.
- **Workflow Interactions**: Uploads compliance documentation; submits permit applications.
- **AI Interactions**: Registration Review Agent checks applications for completeness prior to submit.
- **Trust Concerns**: Government portal might leak client data to competitors.
- **Success Measures**: Permit processing times reduced by 70% for client facilities.

### 1.11 Facility Owner (Regulated Business)

- **Objectives**: Keep the business running legally without administrative delays or excessive costs.
- **Responsibilities**: Pays fees, submits renewal applications, and ensures staff follow regulations.
- **Pain Points**: Unclear regulatory checklists and surprise inspector visits with unverified fine demands.
- **Permissions**: Read access to own facility profile; Write access to submissions.
- **Information Needs**: Exact checklist rules, fee schedules, inspection results, fine statuses.
- **Workflow Interactions**: Views compliance score, pays renewal invoices.
- **AI Interactions**: Citizen/Facility Support Agent answers regulatory questions instantly.
- **Trust Concerns**: Inspectors using biased scoring to extract bribes.
- **Success Measures**: Zero regulatory shutdowns; clear compliance rating visible online.

### 1.12 Facility Compliance Manager

- **Objectives**: Implement correct daily operational procedures to meet compliance targets.
- **Responsibilities**: Inspects facility equipment, logs internal audits, and trains on-site staff.
- **Pain Points**: Keep track of daily compliance tasks across multiple facility sections.
- **Permissions**: Write access to internal logs and uploads for their facility.
- **Information Needs**: Daily tasks, inspector checklists, remediation logs.
- **Workflow Interactions**: Uploads proof of remediation (photos/maintenance logs).
- **AI Interactions**: Receives alerts on potential risks from compliance engine.
- **Trust Concerns**: System alerts might overload internal workflow.
- **Success Measures**: 100% compliance score during government inspections.

### 1.13 Citizen Complainant

- **Objectives**: Report environmental hazards (e.g., wastewater dumps) and see fast resolution.
- **Responsibilities**: Submits photos, videos, and descriptions of local violations.
- **Pain Points**: Filing reports into a black hole with no feedback or status updates.
- **Permissions**: Write access to submit complaint; Read access to their specific complaint status.
- **Information Needs**: Ticket tracking status, actions taken by the agency.
- **Workflow Interactions**: Submits complaint form on public portal; checks status.
- **AI Interactions**: Citizen Support Agent processes the submission and answers general questions.
- **Trust Concerns**: Identity exposure to retaliatory business owners.
- **Success Measures**: Instant tracking code issued; status updates received at milestones.

### 1.14 Platform Support Administrator (GovOS Employee)

- **Objectives**: Monitor system health across all tenants and maintain platform stability.
- **Responsibilities**: Manages global deployments, reviews system logs, and debugs API gateway.
- **Pain Points**: Pinpointing performance bottlenecks in complex multi-tenant environments.
- **Permissions**: Read-write access to system performance infrastructure (no direct tenant data access).
- **Information Needs**: API response latencies, server error counts, Vertex AI token consumption.
- **Workflow Interactions**: Monitors performance graphs, adjusts scaling policies.
- **AI Interactions**: Uses anomaly detection scripts to flag abnormal token or cost surges.
- **Trust Concerns**: Preventing any data leaks across tenant boundaries.
- **Success Measures**: 99.99% system uptime; zero cross-tenant database leaks.

## 2. Operational User Journeys (17 Journeys)

Here we define the core operations step-by-step. Each journey outlines happy and failure paths, human approval gates, and AI interactions.

```
[Citizen Complaint] ──> [AI Triage] ──> [Case Assignment] ──> [Field Inspection]
                                                                      │
[Audit Ledger] <── [Case Closure] <── [Remediation] <── [Report Approval & Action]
```

---

### Journey 1: Complaint Submission and Triage

- **Actor**: Citizen Complainant / Complaint Intake Officer / Complaint Triage Agent.
- **Happy Path**:
  1.  Citizen accesses the public tenant portal and uploads photos of a car wash dumping wastewater onto a street, including GPS coordinates.
  2.  The Complaint Triage Agent parses the text, extracts the category "Wastewater Discharge", maps the GPS coordinates to a facility registry address, and sets the priority score to "High" due to water contamination risk.
  3.  A case file is automatically initialized, and the Citizen receives an SMS tracking code.
- **Failure Path**: The uploaded photo contains no geolocation data and the text description is vague ("smelly water on the road").
- **Escalation Path**: System assigns the complaint to the Complaint Intake Officer's manual queue for location validation.
- **Human Approval**: Director must approve the conversion of the triaged complaint into a formal case.
- **AI Handoff**: Triage Agent hands off the structured JSON payload to the Case Management service.
- **Notification**: Citizen receives SMS/Email updates when the case is initialized.
- **UX/UI Constraints**: Web portal optimized for low-bandwidth, mobile-responsive layout (<50KB page size).

---

### Journey 2: Complaint-to-Case Conversion

- **Actor**: Director / Case Management System / Workflow Coordination Agent.
- **Happy Path**:
  1.  Director opens the triaged complaints dashboard and reviews the "High Priority Wastewater Discharge" complaint.
  2.  Director clicks "Create Case". The system maps the complaint to the target facility in the registry.
  3.  A formal Case ID (`CASE-2026-0089`) is generated and linked to the Facility ID.
- **Failure Path**: The complaint is determined to be malicious or a duplicate of an existing active case.
- **Escalation Path**: Director marks the complaint as "Duplicate" or "Archived" and inputs the reasoning, notifying the complainant.
- **Human Approval**: Requires Director signature to authorize case initialization.
- **AI Handoff**: Triage Agent metadata is merged into the Case entity.
- **Notification**: Notification Engine triggers an internal dashboard alert for the department supervisor.
- **UX/UI Constraints**: Simple screen displaying the incoming complaint data side-by-side with matched facility profiles.

---

### Journey 3: Inspection Planning

- **Actor**: Supervisor / Risk Assessment Agent.
- **Happy Path**:
  1.  Supervisor reviews the active cases queue at the start of the week.
  2.  Risk Assessment Agent scans the facility history and calculates the facility's risk score (increasing it due to the recent wastewater complaint).
  3.  The agent automatically suggests an inspection task, selecting the "Wastewater compliance template" for the inspector.
- **Failure Path**: The facility profile has incorrect metadata, making the risk score calculation unreliable.
- **Escalation Path**: The supervisor manually overrides the risk score and selects the inspection template.
- **Human Approval**: Supervisor must review and authorize the generated inspection task.
- **AI Handoff**: Risk Assessment Agent writes the target template selection and risk metrics to the inspection record.
- **Notification**: Slack or system dashboard notification sent to the supervisor.
- **UX/UI Constraints**: Accessible calendar and map layout indicating facility risk values.

---

### Journey 4: Inspection Assignment

- **Actor**: Supervisor / Inspector / Inspection Planning Agent.
- **Happy Path**:
  1.  Supervisor views the inspection planning map.
  2.  The Inspection Planning Agent analyzes the geographical locations of inspectors and their current workloads.
  3.  The agent suggests assigning the task to Inspector Marcus, who is scheduled to be in that specific district.
  4.  Supervisor clicks "Confirm Assignment".
- **Failure Path**: The assigned inspector reports sick or is unavailable on the day.
- **Escalation Path**: System automatically re-routes the task to the nearest inspector.
- **Human Approval**: Supervisor signs off on the route and inspector assignment.
- **AI Handoff**: Planning Agent outputs the optimized routing queue to the inspector's mobile interface.
- **Notification**: Inspector receives a push notification and system email with the details.
- **UX/UI Constraints**: Gantt chart interface showing workloads and travel routes.

---

### Journey 5: Field Inspection

- **Actor**: Inspector.
- **Happy Path**:
  1.  Inspector Marcus arrives at the facility and opens the mobile web app.
  2.  The app uses the browser GPS to confirm Marcus is on-site before unlocking the "Wastewater Compliance Checklist".
  3.  Marcus answers questions (e.g., "Is the oil separator functional? Yes") and adds photos of the clean separator.
  4.  Marcus submits the checklist.
- **Failure Path**: Mobile signal is lost during the inspection.
- **Escalation Path**: The web application buffers the data locally in IndexedDB and uploads once a connection is re-established.
- **Human Approval**: Inspector certifies that all questions are answered truthfully.
- **AI Handoff**: Checked questionnaire is submitted to the Evidence Analysis Agent.
- **Notification**: Supervisor dashboard updates to show the inspector is on-site.
- **UX/UI Constraints**: Large touch targets, high contrast (usable in direct sunlight), and light page weight.

---

### Journey 6: Evidence Upload

- **Actor**: Inspector / Evidence Engine.
- **Happy Path**:
  1.  Inspector uploads three high-resolution JPG photos of the separator through the mobile interface.
  2.  The system captures GPS, timestamp, and device metadata, generating a cryptographic hash (SHA-256) of each file.
  3.  The photos are saved in Cloud Storage using a secure, tenant-isolated path.
- **Failure Path**: Upload fails midway due to a poor network connection.
- **Escalation Path**: The app automatically retries uploading chunks of the file until successful.
- **Human Approval**: None (automated system process).
- **AI Handoff**: Hashed file metadata is passed to the Evidence Analysis Agent.
- **Notification**: Real-time progress bar on the inspector's screen.
- **UX/UI Constraints**: Image preview with status indicators showing upload and hashing progress.

---

### Journey 7: AI Evidence Review

- **Actor**: Evidence Analysis Agent.
- **Happy Path**:
  1.  The Evidence Analysis Agent scans the uploaded images.
  2.  The agent confirms that the images match the checklist answers (e.g., confirming the separator is present and visible).
  3.  The agent flags no signs of tampering and calculates a confidence score of 95%.
- **Failure Path**: The photo is blurry, dark, or contains an incorrect object (e.g., an inspector took a photo of the sky instead of the separator).
- **Escalation Path**: Agent flags the image as "Low Quality" and requests the inspector to retake the photo.
- **Human Approval**: None (automated review; if flagged, the inspector must review and upload a replacement).
- **AI Handoff**: Structured analysis results are written to the case database record.
- **Notification**: Toast message displayed on the inspector's mobile interface.
- **UX/UI Constraints**: Image modal highlighting detected regions of interest (e.g., highlighting where the oil separator was located).

---

### Journey 8: Inspection Report Approval

- **Actor**: Inspector / Supervisor / Report Drafting Agent.
- **Happy Path**:
  1.  Report Drafting Agent compiles the checklist responses, evidence analyses, and facility history.
  2.  The agent drafts a formal Inspection Report PDF and places it in the supervisor's queue.
  3.  Supervisor reviews the document, makes one minor edit, and clicks "Approve and Sign".
- **Failure Path**: Supervisor rejects the report because the inspector's field findings are insufficient.
- **Escalation Path**: Report is returned to the inspector's task queue with feedback comments.
- **Human Approval**: Supervisor's digital signature is required to finalize the report.
- **AI Handoff**: Report Drafting Agent generates the PDF and stores it in Cloud Storage.
- **Notification**: Inspector is notified if the report is rejected; Facility Owner is notified once approved.
- **UX/UI Constraints**: Side-by-side view comparing the PDF draft with the raw checklist answers.

---

### Journey 9: Compliance Recommendation

- **Actor**: Compliance Recommendation Agent / Compliance Officer.
- **Happy Path**:
  1.  The finalized inspection report shows a critical wastewater violation.
  2.  Compliance Recommendation Agent scans the tenant's environmental policy rules and recommends: "Issue a 14-day remediation warning and assign a $500 fine".
  3.  Compliance Officer accepts the recommendation.
- **Failure Path**: The AI recommendation does not align with local laws or templates.
- **Escalation Path**: Compliance Officer overrides the recommendation, selecting a manual fine and timeline.
- **Human Approval**: Compliance Officer must review and approve the recommended penalty.
- **AI Handoff**: Recommended parameters are used to generate the legal notice.
- **Notification**: Compliance Officer dashboard receives the notice task.
- **UX/UI Constraints**: Interactive checklist outlining the relevant environmental regulations.

---

### Journey 10: Remediation Tracking

- **Actor**: Facility Owner / Compliance Officer / Workflow Agent.
- **Happy Path**:
  1.  Facility Owner receives a warning notice via email.
  2.  Owner repairs the oil separator, uploads photos of the fix, and submits the proof through the facility portal.
  3.  Compliance Officer reviews the proof, marks the violation as resolved, and updates the compliance score.
- **Failure Path**: The facility owner ignores the notice, and the 14-day deadline passes.
- **Escalation Path**: The system escalates the case status to "Non-Compliant" and forwards it to the enforcement team.
- **Human Approval**: Compliance Officer must sign off to certify the remediation is complete.
- **AI Handoff**: Evidence submitted by the facility owner is processed by the Evidence Analysis Agent.
- **Notification**: Email and SMS alerts sent to the facility owner 5 days, 3 days, and 1 day before the deadline.
- **UX/UI Constraints**: Client portal showing a countdown timer and upload zones.

---

### Journey 11: Case Escalation

- **Actor**: Legal Officer / Director.
- **Happy Path**:
  1.  Case status changes to "Non-Compliant" after remediation deadline passes.
  2.  System compiles all case history, evidence, and ledger events into a single zip archive.
  3.  Director clicks "Refer to Legal".
- **Failure Path**: Case files are incomplete or evidence hashes do not match.
- **Escalation Path**: System raises an alarm to the system administrator to verify data integrity.
- **Human Approval**: Director must approve the legal escalation.
- **AI Handoff**: Document Intelligence Agent compiles the case dossier.
- **Notification**: Legal team receives a priority email.
- **UX/UI Constraints**: High-security approval interface with mandatory double-factor authentication.

---

### Journey 12: Case Closure

- **Actor**: Director / Auditor.
- **Happy Path**:
  1.  Wastewater issues are remediated and verified.
  2.  Director clicks "Close Case".
  3.  The system marks the case as closed, logs the final status on the ledger, and notifies the complainant.
- **Failure Path**: A case is closed without necessary signatures or unresolved complaints remaining.
- **Escalation Path**: System rejects the closure and displays the missing dependencies.
- **Human Approval**: Director signature required for case closure.
- **AI Handoff**: Case data is locked; historical metrics are calculated by the reporting engine.
- **Notification**: Complainant and Facility Owner receive final case summaries.
- **UX/UI Constraints**: Confirmation dialog showing summary audit checklist.

---

### Journey 13: Facility Registration

- **Actor**: Facility Owner / Environmental Consultant.
- **Happy Path**:
  1.  Consultant opens the registry portal to register a new restaurant facility.

  3.  Consultant uploads business registrations and permits, then submits the form.
- **Failure Path**: Consultant fails to upload required documents.
- **Escalation Path**: Form prevents submission and highlights missing documents.
- **Human Approval**: Registration is sent to the agency queue for verification.
- **AI Handoff**: Payload passed to the Registration Review Agent.
- **Notification**: Confirmation email sent to the consultant.
- **UX/UI Constraints**: Step-by-step progress bar showing application stages.

---

### Journey 14: Registration Review

- **AI Handoff**: Agent structures the validation report.
- **Notification**: Applicant receives a request for info.
- **UX/UI Constraints**: Review screen comparing uploaded files with metadata.

  3.  System checks the schema format and applies the new rules.
- **Failure Path**: Uploaded JSON is malformed or invalid.
